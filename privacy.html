Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Applies to wickeddossier.com

Wicked Dossier is an unofficial, fan-made guide and build planner for No Rest for the Wicked, run by one person. This page explains what the site does with your information. The short version: you do not need an account for anything, and if you choose to sign in, the only thing it is used for is saving your builds.

Using the site without an account

The guide, the item database and the Cerim Builder all work without signing in. The builder keeps your current build in your own browser (local storage) so it is there when you come back. That data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Share links contain the build itself inside the link, so anyone you give a link to can see that build. They do not reveal who you are.

Signing in (optional)

You can register with an email address, or log in with Discord or Google, to save builds to your account and open them on other devices. With Discord or Google, the site receives from that provider:

  • your account ID with that provider,
  • your display name, and
  • your email address.

It also stores the builds you choose to save. If you register with an email and password, the password is handled and stored (hashed) by our authentication provider, Supabase, and is never visible to us. We do not receive your Discord or Google password, and we do not read your messages, contacts, files or anything else from Discord or Google.

Publishing a build (optional)

From My builds you can choose to publish a saved build to the Community Builds page. A published build, the one-line description you write and the display name you enter are shown publicly to anyone visiting the site, and anyone signed in can like or report it. Nothing is published unless you choose it, and you can unpublish at any time from My builds, which removes it from the page immediately. Reports are seen only by the site owner.

The author name on a published build links to an author page that lists all of that person’s public builds, along with the display name and short bio they choose to set under account menu → Edit profile. That page is on by default and can be turned off there at any time; when it is off, the name on your builds is plain text and nothing links to you.

How your information is used

Only to let you sign in and to load the builds you saved. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used to build a profile of you. We do not send marketing email.

Visitor statistics

The site counts page views with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. It records the page visited, the referring site, browser and screen size, country, and a short-lived hash used only to tell repeat visits apart for the day. It sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses and collects nothing that identifies you personally; the numbers are used only to see which parts of the site people find useful.

Who handles it

Sign-in and saved builds are handled by Supabase, which stores the data on our behalf, together with the sign-in provider you pick (Discord or Google). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. The Media page embeds official YouTube videos; YouTube may set its own cookies when you press play, under Google's policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and every saved build yourself at any time: sign in, open your name in the menu, choose My builds, then Delete my account. This removes your stored name, email and builds. You can also ask for deletion by email (below). Disconnecting the app in your Discord or Google account settings stops it from receiving anything further.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
